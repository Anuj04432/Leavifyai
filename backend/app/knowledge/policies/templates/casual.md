# Casual & Personal Leave Policy

## 1. Entitlement & Accrual
Full-time employees are credited with 12 days of paid Casual Leave at the beginning of each calendar year.

## 2. Notice Guidelines
- For leaves of 1 to 2 consecutive days, notice of at least 24 hours is required.
- For leave requests exceeding 3 consecutive days, prior written approval from the department head is mandatory.

## 3. Financial & Payroll Reconciliation
- Casual leave is fully paid.
- Zero deduction applies to monthly basic salary or fixed allowances under Rule 2.4.

## 4. Carryover Rules
- A maximum of 3 unused casual leave days may be carried over into the subsequent calendar year.
