# Annual Paid Vacation Policy

## 1. Entitlement & Usage
- Employees accrue 15 days of paid annual vacation per full calendar year.
- Minimum advance notice of 7 calendar days is required for planning resource coverage.

## 2. Block Leave Guidelines
- Up to 14 consecutive calendar days can be taken in a single block upon prior manager sign-off.
- Weekends and statutory public holidays falling within the leave range are strictly excluded from balance consumption.

## 3. Carryover & Encashment
- A maximum of 10 days can be rolled over to the following year.
