# Medical & Sick Leave Policy

## 1. Entitlement & Notification
- All employees receive 10 days of paid Sick Leave annually.
- Unplanned sick leave does not require advance notice (notice_period = 0), but manager notification before 10:00 AM on the day of absence is required.

## 2. Documentation Requirements
- Medical certificate is required from an authorized healthcare provider for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive working days (from day 3 onwards).

## 3. Financial & Payroll Protection
- Approved medical leave incurs zero salary loss. Full basic pay and allowances are preserved under Section 3.1.
