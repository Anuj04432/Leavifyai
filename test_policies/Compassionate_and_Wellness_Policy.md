# Mental Wellness & Compassionate Leave Policy

## 1. Mental Health Recharge Days
Every employee is entitled to **7 fully paid mental wellness recharge days** each calendar year. These days can be taken spontaneously without requiring advance notice or doctor's prescription when an employee feels burnt out or overwhelmed.

### Rules and Guidelines:
- **Zero Notice Required**: Can be requested on the morning of absence by notifying the supervisor or sending a quick email.
- **No Medical Certificate**: Unlike statutory sick leaves, no proof or physician diagnosis is ever demanded.
- **Maximum Duration**: Up to 3 consecutive days per instance. If more than 3 days are required, standard medical leave guidelines apply.

## 2. Bereavement & Compassionate Leave
In the unfortunate event of the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, grandparent), employees receive:
- **5 consecutive paid days** for local ceremonies.
- **10 consecutive paid days** if travel outside the home state or country is required.

## 3. Voluntary Community Service Day
Employees are granted **1 additional paid volunteer day** each fiscal quarter to participate in accredited charitable, environmental, or community initiatives.
